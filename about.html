Pamela Scott

About

Pamela Scott is a singer, songwriter, and producer who is making waves in the music industry with her unique rock sound. Hailing from Rivers State, Nigeria, Pamela has carved out a niche for herself in the rock genre, showcasing her powerful vocals, guitar skills, and introspective lyrics.

She has released an impressive EP titled Who I Am (2019), which gives a glimpse of her journey in finding her identity. In addition to her EP, Pamela has also released six singles; Rest (2025), Soldier (2021), Love Equals Jesus (2018), Flames Of Love> (2017), Teach Me (2016), Habitation (2015), — each one demonstrating the love of God and hope.

She has also been featured on international stages in London, Liverpool, and Manchester.

Pamela Scott is also a member of Hillsong Liverpool Worship Team.

Pamela Scott is quoted to say “I sing the truth because it sets one free”. Inspired by the revelation of Jesus Christ, Pamela Scott is on a mission to help people discover their identity, because life becomes meaningful when you know who you are.
