Is this event really free?

Yes, entry is completely free. Registration is required because seating is limited.

Please let us know via WhatsApp so we can free up your spot for someone on the waitlist.

Is my spot guaranteed?

Spots are held until 2:00PM on the day of the show. After that, unclaimed seats are released to walk-ins.
